We provide in this repository an indication when phrases were
extracted from / exist in 'Deutsch im Blick' or 'Grimm Grammar'.

--------------------------------------------------
'Deutsch in Blick' and 'Grimm Grammar' attribution
--------------------------------------------------
Deutsch im Blick (2nd edition, 2017) and Grimm Grammar are a free
online first-year German textbook and companion grammar authored by
Dr Zsuzsanna Abrams and published by the Center for Open Educational
Resources and Language Learning (COERLL) at the University of Texas-Austin.
This repository primarily consists of vocabulary and matching audio files (when available)
from the end of each chapter in Deutsch im Blick. Selected elements of Grimm Grammar
or vocabulary introduced in the chapter text are also included.

URL of the Deutsch im Blick homepage: https://coerll.utexas.edu/dib/

URL of the Grimm Grammar homepage: https://coerll.utexas.edu/gg/

This derivative work is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. This is a modification of the
original work. This repository is not affiliated with or endorsed by Dr. Abrams or COERLL.

The files contain a field with the relevant chapter(s) from
which the headword(s) for each note was taken.

- We usually attempt to present one note per sense / meaning. This means we sometimes
 combine words presented in different chapters in the vocabulary list or grammar pages
 on a single note. This also means a word that might be presented on a single line
 in the textbook or grammar generates multiple notes. For example 'das Schloss' is
 on a separate note for 'lock' and 'castle', even though both translations appear on
 a single line in the Chapter 6 vocabulary list.

 The source location(s) for the words are given in the `dib_chapter_list` variable.

 Although the above bullet is the goal, we sometimes leave two German words on
 separate notes, even if their meaning or some of their meanings are the same.
 One reason for this is to keep individual cards short.
